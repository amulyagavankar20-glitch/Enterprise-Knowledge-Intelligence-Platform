What is the reimbursement limit and submission window for home office equipment under the updated remote work policy?

auto_awesome
Synthesized Intelligence Deterministic RAG Extraction • Confidence: 99.4%

Under Section 4.2 of the Remote Work & Equipment Policy, full-time employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement of up to $750 USD for approved ergonomic equipment (monitors, chairs, standing desk converters). All reimbursement requests must be submitted via the Concur procurement portal within 60 calendar days of purchase with itemized receipts attached. Purchases exceeding $750 require pre-approval from department VP.

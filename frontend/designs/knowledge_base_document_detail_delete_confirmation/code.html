1. Purpose & Scope

The purpose of this Equipment & Workspace Policy is to provide remote, hybrid, and distributed team members with the necessary infrastructure, hardware tools, and ergonomic support to conduct their professional responsibilities safely, efficiently, and with sustained operational continuity. This policy applies uniformly to all permanent full-time personnel across international corporate entities.

CHUNK #002 • pg. 1-2 Token Span: [385 - 812]

2. Eligibility & Classification

Employees operating under verified Remote Work Agreements (designated as code R-FULL or HY-3) become eligible for corporate provisioning upon completion of identity verification through enterprise onboarding. Contractors, part-time seasonal interns, and advisory consultants are addressed under Schedule D appendix provisions.

CHUNK #003 • pg. 2 Token Span: [813 - 1240]

3. Core Hardware Stipends

Every authorized employee is supplied directly via Global IT Procurement with a baseline workstation configuration consisting of: (a) a tier-1 enterprise laptop (macOS or Linux enterprise image), (b) dual 27-inch 4K high-density color-calibrated monitors, and (c) standardized universal biometric docking accessories. Replacements are governed by a mandatory 36-month refresh schedule.

4. Home Workspace Allowance

Full-time employees are entitled to a one-time reimbursement of up to $750 for ergonomic peripherals, sit-stand motorized desks, certified lumbar task chairs, and dedicated noise-cancelling telephony equipment. Expenses must be submitted via ExpenseHub within 90 calendar days of initial hire verification.
